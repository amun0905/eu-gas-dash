Data sources

SourceWhat we useAccess & terms
GIE AGSI+ Daily gas in storage, fill %, injection and withdrawal for the EU and each country (Storage tab). Free API with a personal key. Data may be reused; GIE must be credited as the source.
GIE ALSI Daily LNG send-out, tank inventory and declared capacities by country and by terminal (LNG tab). Same key and terms as AGSI+.
GIE IIP Urgent market messages from storage, LNG and transmission operators (Events tab). Same key and terms as AGSI+.
ENTSOG Transparency Platform Daily physical flows and nominations at selected points (Flows tab, LNG cross-check), TSO urgent market messages (Events tab) and CMP auction premiums (Auctions tab). Free public API, no key.
National Gas Data Portal GB NTS supply by source (incl. Langeled from Norway), demand and seasonal normal demand (Flows tab). Free public data. Volumes converted from mcm at 10.97 GWh/mcm.
Open-Meteo Daily mean temperature for eight cities, for heating degree days (Storage tab). Free for non-commercial use, CC BY 4.0.

Prices are not shown: there is no free official API for TTF or other hub prices. Volumes (mcm) use 10.55 kWh/m³ for continental data; GB volumes are National Gas's own published figures.
